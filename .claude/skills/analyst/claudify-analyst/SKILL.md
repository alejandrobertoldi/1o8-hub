# Claudify Analyst (the product)

One line: the research engine that puts its predictions on the record, and grades
them. It wins on ACCOUNTABILITY, not intelligence; every AI does research, this
one shows its work, names what it could not find, and keeps score in a
tamper-evident ledger.

## What the buyer gets

- **The engine** (installs into their own Claude Code, runs on their key): a
  claim-evidence graph where belief is COMPUTED from independence-weighted
  evidence, never asserted; ingest gates that quarantine fabricated numbers,
  wrong-entity citations, and hostile pages; ACH reasoning where the
  least-disconfirmed hypothesis wins; retrieval-adequacy scoring that says when
  the engine did not look hard enough and caps the verdict accordingly.
- **The team**: a strategist that frames decisions, a sensor, an
  evidence-integrator, 12 research specialists, a bull, a bear, and a red-team
  that argue from isolated contexts, a synthesizer, and an analyst that writes
  hash-chained predictions and grades them against reality with proper scores.
- **The commands**: staged `/research` (frame for under a dollar, research one
  crux at a time, verdict only at coverage) plus the presets: `/research-brief`,
  `/market-size`, `/competitor-teardown`, `/customer-research`,
  `/pricing-research`, `/due-diligence`, `/trend-radar`, `/research-report`,
  `/research-learn`, `/research-audit`, `/market-watch`.
- **The deliverable**: the Conviction Dossier (founder template) or Diligence
  Dossier (investor template): a rendered, typeset artifact with the verdict,
  the cruxes with numeric flip-conditions, likelihood bands + computed
  confidence, the ACH matrix, the assumptions ledger, the full evidence chains,
  the quarantine section, and the sources-we-expected-but-did-not-find ledger.
- **The discipline skills**: analytic-tradecraft + quality-and-calibration.

## The honesty features (the actual differentiation; never soften in copy)

1. It refuses a GO/NOGO unless YOU sign the risk framing (your loss ratio, never
   the machine's).
2. A cheap partial run can never masquerade as the answer ("resolved 1 of 3
   cruxes, not yet a verdict").
3. A one-source claim can never print the same confidence as a corroborated one
   (single-origin hard cap), and laundered citations collapse to one vote.
4. It tells you what it could NOT find, and a weak retrieval caps its own verdict.
5. Its predictions are dated, falsifiable, hash-chained, with the grading
   criterion frozen at write time, and graded honestly: unresolved calls are
   counted, never dropped, and below 20 resolved it says "uncalibrated" first.

## Usage and cost (say this plainly)

Staged by default so a run never threatens the buyer's token allowance: stage 1
frames for well under a dollar; each crux is an explicit opt-in (~$1-2); a full
three-crux run with verdict lands around $4-7 and 25-45 minutes on the buyer's
own key. Depth is opt-in; the discipline (gates, bands, honesty) is never
skippable. Heavy users are steered toward an API key or Max plan; the cost
estimate is always shown before spending.

## The hard boundaries (house rules, absolute)

- Never a public negative named-entity teardown, from us or positioned as a
  product use.
- Every dossier carries the not-investment-advice line; the engine is decision
  support.
- No em dashes anywhere. No money-back guarantee language. Claudify identity only in public.
- Buyer honesty: their numbers are uncalibrated below n=20 resolved and the
  product says so; never imply a fitted track record a buyer does not have.
