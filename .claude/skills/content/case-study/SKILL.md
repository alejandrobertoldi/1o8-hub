# Case Study

The output is the finished story: before, intervention, after, with the numbers in a
table and quotes placed where they carry the argument. Never invent results; if a
number is missing, ask, or mark the claim qualitative.

## Procedure

1. Collect the facts: customer name and context (or an approved anonymous label),
   the situation before, why they chose you, what was actually done, the measured
   results with timeframes, and any usable verbatim quotes.
2. Structure: a results-first headline, a 3-line summary box (customer, problem,
   headline result), The challenge (their world before, specific pains), The
   approach (what happened, in plain sequence), The results (table: metric, before,
   after, timeframe), a closing quote, and a CTA.
3. Pull quotes: 2 minimum, verbatim from the customer, each under 30 words, placed
   after the claim they support. Never paraphrase into quotation marks.
4. Numbers rule: every "after" figure has a timeframe and a source; percentage
   improvements also show the absolutes.

## Worked example (fictional excerpt)

Headline: "Fernway Cycles cut quote turnaround from 4 days to 6 hours."

| Metric | Before | After | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quote turnaround | 4 days | 6 hours | within 8 weeks |
| Quotes sent/month | 31 | 74 | month 3 |
| Close rate | 22% (7 of 31) | 27% (20 of 74) | month 3 |

Pull quote placement: after the turnaround claim, "We stopped losing jobs to
whoever answered first. Now that is us." All names and numbers fictional.

## Output contract

Deliver: headline with the strongest number, summary box, The challenge, The
approach, The results table (metric, before, after, timeframe), two or more placed
pull quotes, and a CTA. Absolutes shown alongside percentages; anything estimated
or anonymized is labeled as such.
