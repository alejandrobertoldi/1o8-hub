# /pricing-research

Thin preset over the Claudify Analyst engine: price-point and pricing-model
analysis. `/pricing-research "<product>"` = `/research --preset=pricing` with the
strategist directed to:
- frame the DECISION as the price/model choice (the options are price points or
  models, not GO/NOGO), with the loss ladder elicited as under-pricing vs
  over-pricing regret,
- anchor on comparables' LIVE pricing pages (the recorded rule: review sites carry
  stale pricing; check the primary), each price a dated claim with a short
  half-life,
- staff pricing-strategy + competitor specialists; require the three-scenario
  framing on any LTV:CAC or margin claim (the cherry-pick tripwire enforces it).
Output: the priced options with the flip-points between them. No em dashes.
