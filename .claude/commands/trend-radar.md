# /trend-radar

Thin preset over the Claudify Analyst engine: an industry trend and regulatory scan
with timing verdicts. `/trend-radar "<domain>"` = `/research --preset=market`
scoped to trend claims, with the strategist directed to:
- express every trend as a dated, falsifiable claim with a short half-life (30-90
  days) and a timing verdict (early / on-time / late) rather than a vibe,
- deliberately search both growth AND decline signals (the trend specialist's
  standing guardrail), flagging any survey without disclosed methodology,
- ledger the 3-5 highest-VOI trend claims as predictions with near-term resolves_by
  dates: trend calls are the cheapest way to build the public track record.
Output: the trend claims table with timing verdicts and the signposts to watch.
No em dashes.
