# /research-brief

Thin preset over the Claudify Analyst engine: the fast lane for one question.
`/research-brief "<question>"` = `/research` STAGE 1 ONLY, in EVIDENCE-MAP mode by
default (no framing card unless offered), stage-1 budget (well under one dollar):
the decision frame, the MECE tree with cruxes tagged, the hypotheses, and the
ranked frontier, with NO evidence collection. The output tells you what the
question actually hinges on and what stage 2 would cost per crux, which for many
questions is the whole value. Never presented as a verdict (the coverage state
makes that structural). No em dashes.
