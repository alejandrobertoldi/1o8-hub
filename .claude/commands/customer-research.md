# /customer-research

Thin preset over the Claudify Analyst engine: demand, willingness-to-pay, and
jobs-to-be-done synthesis. `/customer-research "<segment or product>"` =
`/research --preset=venture` scoped to the demand branch, with the strategist
directed to:
- make WTP the crux (it is the classic venture crux and the least-searchable one),
- prefer primary demand evidence (interviews, signup/pricing behavior, cohort data)
  over stated-preference surveys, and label survey evidence with its n and method,
- staff the customer-demand specialist first, unit-economics second,
- score "would pay" claims against the blue-ocean vs dead-zone dual reading.
Output: the demand evidence map with the one cheapest experiment that would settle
the WTP crux. No em dashes.
