# /competitor-teardown

Thin preset over the Claudify Analyst engine: a deep single-competitor (or set)
teardown. `/competitor-teardown "<who>"` = `/research --preset=competitor` with the
strategist directed to:
- build the issue tree around: what they actually sell, who pays and why, their
  moat by the 7 Powers, their weaknesses that are exploitable NOW, and their likely
  next moves (each next-move a falsifiable, dated claim for the ledger),
- staff the existing competitor + moat + unit-economics specialists,
- treat all self-reported competitor metrics with the +/-30% rule, and absence of
  visible competition as a dual-reading finding, never as reassurance.
PRIVATE dossier only: the hard rule barring public negative named-entity teardowns
applies to anything that leaves the machine. No em dashes.
