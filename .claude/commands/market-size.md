# /market-size

Thin preset over the Claudify Analyst engine: a standalone TAM/SAM/SOM sizing with
scope discipline. `/market-size "<market>"` = `/research "<how big is market X,
honestly scoped>" --preset=market` with the strategist directed to:
- make SCOPE the first crux (the recorded failure pattern: 3-6x disagreements are
  scope definitions, not measurement noise; see G-aivideo and G-aiart in the golden set),
- elicit every size as a fractile interval (p10/p50/p90), never a point,
- require a primary anchor (the ESC quantity rule) and both top-down and bottom-up
  claims where the budget allows.
Output: the evidence map with intervals and the scope ledger; the full dossier only
when a signed decision frame accompanies the question. No em dashes.
