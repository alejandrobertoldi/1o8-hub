1. Where to Log Hours

Hours → Log hours

From the top navigation bar in the MF Hub, click Log hours. This opens the User Hours page, where the entry form sits at the top of the screen and your existing entries are listed underneath.

2. Logging an Entry

Work left to right across the form bar. Every entry needs a date, a client, a category, a start time and a finish — then click Log hours.

1
Date
The date field defaults to today. If you are catching up on an earlier day, click the field and pick the correct date from the calendar.
2
Client
Open the Client dropdown and select the client you were working for. The list is searchable, so start typing the name rather than scrolling.
Client work
Select that client.
Internal work — including internal calls and meetings
Select Market Foundry. Internal time still gets logged.
3
Category
Choose the category that matches the type of work — for example PPC, SEO or Design. Pick the one that best describes what you actually did during that block of time.
Missing a category?
If the work you did doesn't fit any of the categories in the list, message Manu and request the category be added.
4
Start time
Set the hour and minutes you started, using the two dropdowns (HH : MM). For example, 11:30.
5
End time or duration
There are two ways to record when the block finished. Use whichever you find quicker — the result is the same.
Method How to switch What to enter
End time Default view The clock time you finished, e.g. 12:00
Duration Click Use duration Hours as a decimal, e.g. 0.5 for 30 minutes, 1.5 for 90 minutes
Clicking Use duration swaps the End dropdowns for a single Hours box, labelled with the finish time it works out to (e.g. Hours (ends 09:00)). To go back, click Use end time.
6
Note
In the What did you work on? field, write a short, specific description of the task, or the Asana task name. The field is labelled optional in the form, but a note is expected on every entry — write notes a client could read.
7
Log hours
Click the Log hours button. The entry appears immediately in the table below the form.
If the button won't submit
The button stays disabled until the entry is valid. Check that you have set the date, the client, the category, and the start time plus either an end time or a duration that produces a real block of time.

3. Reviewing Your Logged Hours

The entries table

Everything you have logged appears in the table underneath the form, showing the date, client, category, time (start–end), hours and note for each entry.

To change an entry, click the cell you want to fix — the row opens for editing in place, with ✓ to save and × to cancel. The trash icon at the end of the row deletes the entry.

Your hours calendar

Between the form and the table, the Your hours calendar shows each day as a coloured cell (3, 6 or 12 months). Click a day to filter the table to that date only. Click the date chip above the table to clear it and go back to the week or month you were viewing.

Totals and filters

Above the table, the This Week, Last Week, This Month, Last Month and All Time presets re-total your logged hours for that span — use these to check nothing is missing before the end of the week. You can also narrow the list with the All clients filter and the Search notes box. Download, next to search, exports a spreadsheet without changing the table you are looking at.

4. Downloading a Spreadsheet

Export your own logged hours for a date range as an Excel file (.xlsx). The file is named hours_YYYY-MM-DD_YYYY-MM-DD.xlsx from the range you picked. Nobody else's hours are included.

1
Open the download dialog
Above the table, next to Search notes, click Download.
2
Choose a date range
The dialog opens on the range the table is currently showing. Change it with a preset, or pick From and To yourself. From must be on or before To.
Presets
This week, Last week, This month, or Last month.
Custom dates
Click the From or To field and pick a date from the calendar.
3
Download spreadsheet
Click the orange Download spreadsheet button. Your browser saves the file. The table on the page does not change.

What is in the file

One row per entry: Date, Start, End, Hours (decimal), Duration (HH:MM), Client, Category and Note. Internal time is labelled Market Foundry. The last row is a Total. An empty range still downloads — you get the header and a zero total, so you can see the range had nothing in it.

5. Fixing Overlapping Entries

You cannot be doing two things at the same time, so two entries covering the same minutes are always an error.

What the Hub does when entries overlap
  • ⚠️On save you get a warning naming the clash — “Overlaps an existing entry”, with the client and times of the entry it collides with.
  • 🏷️Every affected row is then badged overlap next to the client name, so the problem stays visible until it is fixed.
  • 💾The entry is still saved — the flag is a prompt for you to correct it, and it must not be left in place.

To fix it

Open whichever entry has the wrong times
Click the Time cell on that row to edit it in place.
Adjust the times
Change the start time, end time or duration so the two blocks no longer cross over.
Save the entry
Click ✓ to confirm the change, or × to abandon it.
Confirm the warning has gone
The overlap badge must disappear from both rows.

6. Rules of Thumb

Five habits that keep hours accurate
  • 📅Log daily. Reconstructing a week from memory is where inaccuracies come from.
  • ✂️One entry per block of work. Split your day by client and by task rather than logging one long entry.
  • ⚠️Never leave an overlap unresolved. Fix it the moment the warning appears.
  • 🏢Internal time still gets logged. Use Market Foundry as the client.
  • ✍️Write notes a client could read. Specific, plain, and about the work.
