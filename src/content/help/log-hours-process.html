Overview

Every 1o8 team member logs their time in the 1o8 Hub, against a client and an Asana project or task. Everything happens on one page — Hours → My timesheet. Hours feed the client estimates, team capacity and the reports in Hours Analytics, so log them every day.

8 h

0.25
Hours are logged in quarter-hour steps (0.25 = 15 minutes)

How often to log — rebuilding a week from memory is where mistakes come from

1. The Timesheet Page

Hours → My timesheet

At the top: the week (arrows to move, This week to come back), your hours this week, and how many workdays in the last 14 days are under 8 hours. Below it, one tile per day with its total; click a day to log or review it. The table at the bottom shows the selected day's entries, newest first (Show whole week lists the full week).

2. Logging an Entry

1
Date and hours
Pick the day (clicking a day tile sets it) and type the hours, e.g. 1.5. Or use Start timer: when you stop it, the hours and date are filled in for you.
2
Client
The client you worked for. Internal work (internal meetings, training, agency ops, sales & new business, PTO) goes to 1O8.
3
Department / type of work
What kind of work it was: Paid Media, Email & SMS, Web Dev, Creative, SEO & Content, Social, Strategy & Planning, Reporting & Analytics, Client Meetings, Internal Meetings, Project Management, Admin Work or Time Off. For team work it narrows the project list to that team's tasks.
4
Project / task
The client's Asana task you worked on (open, or completed in the last 14 days). If there's no Asana task, use the client's general code, CODE00000 (e.g. KSS00000). For 1O8, pick the internal project: General internal work, Internal meetings, Training & learning, Agency ops & admin, Sales & new business, 1o8 Hub, PTO or Company holiday.
5
Notes, then Add
Add a short note if it helps (optional) and click Add. The entry appears in the table and the day's total updates.

3. Editing or Deleting

From the table

Click an entry's pencil (Edit) to load it back into the form, change it and Save (or Cancel). The trash icon deletes it. Fix mistakes the same day: hours feed client estimates and capacity reports.

4. Time Off and Holidays

Holidays: nothing to log

Public holidays on your holiday calendar (US unless Admin → Users says otherwise; the dates are in Admin → Holidays) aren't workdays: they show as Holiday on your timesheet and never count as short, toward expected hours or team capacity.

PTO: request it, then log it

Request days off in Hours → Time off requests so they're approved and show on the team calendar. Then log them on your timesheet: client 1O8, type Time Off, project PTO, 8 hours. That keeps the day from showing as short.
