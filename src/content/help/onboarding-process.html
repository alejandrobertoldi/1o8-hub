Overview

At 1o8, a client is onboarded when its profile in the 1o8 Hub is complete: contract and services, team, contacts, brand voice, brand guidelines, KPIs, data connections and operations. Clients → Client Onboarding shows how complete each active client is; each client's onboarding page lists exactly what's missing and lets you fill it in place.

10
Sections — Basics · Contract · Team · Contacts · Brand Voice · Brand Guidelines · Website & Social · KPIs · Connections · Operations
By service
Only what the client's services need is asked (e.g. Klaviyo only for Email clients)
5
Setup steps — Create client · Assign team · Asana project · Drive folders · Onboarding email

1. Where to Start

Clients → Client Onboarding

One row per active client with its profile percentage. Click a client to open its onboarding page: the SOW / Quote bar on top (the signed scope, where start date and term come from), then the checklist by section, then the setup steps.

2. Completing the Checklist

1
Check the services first
In Contract, the client's services (from the SOW) decide what the rest of the checklist asks for: Email needs Klaviyo and an Email Manager, Paid Media needs Meta / Google Ads, SEO needs keywords and reference posts, and so on. With no services checked, everything is asked.
2
Fill
Each missing item has Fill: simple fields (website, tier, SOW link, Google Ads ID…) are saved right in the popup; the rest link to where they live in the profile (Brand Voice, Brand Guidelines, Main KPIs…). Items read from the profile turn green on their own once filled.
3
Check the manual items
Some things can't be read from the hub — accesses granted to 1o8 (Shopify collaborator, Klaviyo user, Google Ads MCC link) and the welcome email. Use Check → Mark as done once they're done.
4
Not applicable
If an item doesn't apply to this client, open its popup and click Not applicable: it leaves the total (Undo N/A brings it back).

3. Setup Steps

Below the checklist

1. Create Client and 2. Assign Team (the 1o8 team roles) → 3. Setup Asana Project: link the client's existing Asana project, or create it from the 1o8 template (Admin → Configuration → Asana project template); it's marked done as soon as the project is linked → 4. Create Drive Folders → 5. Send Onboarding Email.

4. Data Connections

See "Connecting Client Data"

The Connections section (GA4, Meta, Klaviyo, Google Ads, Shopify) is what feeds the client's Main KPIs and reports. The steps for each source are in the Connecting Client Data guide.
