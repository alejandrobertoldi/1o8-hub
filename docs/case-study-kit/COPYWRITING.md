# Case Study Copywriting Guide — market·foundry

How to write every headline, sentence and label in the Seller Central case study templates (High-Level 5-slide and Detailed 14-slide). Read this before filling any `[placeholder]`.

---

## 1. Workflow: data first, words second

1. **Pull the data** (IntentWise / Seller Central / Ads exports) for the full period.
2. **Compute the story numbers** before writing anything:
   - Total ordered sales, units, sessions, page views
   - First month vs last month (growth multiple, e.g. `5.2×`)
   - H1 vs H2 (% change)
   - Peak month and its value
   - Organic vs ad-attributed split (%)
   - Ad spend, ad sales, blended ROAS, ACOS by channel (SP vs SB+SD)
   - Buy Box %, unit-session % (conversion)
   - Top 3 ASINs and their share of revenue
3. **Find the one headline result.** Pick the single most impressive, true number (usually a growth multiple or total). Everything else supports it.
4. **Write the copy** using the rules below. Every claim must trace back to a number from step 2.

Never invent a number. If data is missing or negligible (e.g. Subscribe & Save with 1 subscriber), drop that section instead of padding.

---

## 2. Voice

- **Confident, plain, specific.** Agency talking to a smart client, not a press release.
- **Short.** Headlines 2–7 words. Body lines 1–2 sentences.
- **Numbers do the bragging.** Don't say "amazing growth" — say "5.2× in a year."
- **Active verbs:** launched, grew, built, harvested, bundled, converted, compounded.
- **No filler words:** "leverage", "synergy", "robust", "cutting-edge", "unlock", "journey".
- **No emoji. No exclamation marks.**
- British/US spelling: follow the client's market (Pura used "flavour", "catalogue").

---

## 3. Headline formula

Headlines are two beats: **setup (white) + payoff (orange accent)**.

```
[Plain statement.] <span accent>[Sharp payoff.]</span>
```

| Slide | Pattern | Example (Pura) |
|---|---|---|
| Cover | Claim. / Result. | "One launch. **5.2× in a year.**" |
| Challenge | The hard thing, framed as a mission | "Launch a brand nobody is **searching for yet.**" |
| Results | Period + count | "One launch year, **six proof points.**" |
| Approach | Count + method | "Five levers, **pulled in sequence.**" |
| Product mix | What concentrates | "Three flavours **carry the fizz.**" |
| Demand curve | Journey | "From zero to **$64K**, steadily." |
| Creative | Contrast | "Not a product. **The brand.**" |
| CTA band | Imperative, uppercase | "EVERY IMAGE EARNS THE CLICK." |

Rules:
- The accent part is where the number or the insight lives.
- A little category wordplay is allowed once or twice per deck ("carry the fizz" for soda) — never forced.
- Brief slide uses three stacked lines: "New brand. / Crowded aisle. / Zero base." — three 2-word facts that define the difficulty.

---

## 4. Body sentence formula

**Context → action → result with a number.**

> "PuraSoda arrived on Amazon with real-cane-sugar sodas and no search demand to ride. Our mandate: stand up the full Seller Central engine — catalogue, creative and PPC — and prove a repeatable growth curve in year one."

> "A $1.4K first month grew into a ~$7K monthly run-rate — a 5.2× ramp, with H2 up 62% over H1."

Rules:
- One idea per sentence. Use an em dash (—) to attach the proof.
- Lead with the situation, end with the number.
- Cite the source period once per slide in the subtitle/topbar, not in every line.
- When a sentence explains *why*, name the lever: "steepening from January as listings, PPC and variety packs compounded."

---

## 5. KPI labels and context lines

- **Label:** 2–4 words, noun phrase, sentence case → "Ordered sales", "Buy Box share", "Ad-attributed sales".
- **Context line:** qualifies the number in ≤6 words → "First 12 months on Seller Central", "Featured-offer share, full year", "On $28.5K spend".
- Long labels split onto two lines with `<br>` (e.g. "Ordered sales<br>Yr 1").
- Pair every $ metric with a ratio where possible (ROAS, % share) — ratios read as skill, raw $ reads as size.

---

## 6. Approach pillars (5)

Each pillar = **Name (2–3 words) + one line of what we did, with a proof point if available.**

```
Retail-ready listings — Full 8-image sets, SEO-tuned copy and A+ on every hero flavour.
Launch PPC            — SP-led keyword and auto campaigns, harvested weekly into exact-match winners.
Brand building        — Brand Story, SB video and a multi-page storefront for the whole range.
Variety packs         — Bundled hero flavours into discovery packs — instant top-five sellers.
Retail basics         — Pricing, inventory and Buy Box discipline — 98.9% featured-offer share.
```

Order them in the sequence they were pulled; the intro line explains how they compound ("Each lever feeds the next…").

---

## 7. Framing weak numbers honestly

Never hide or fake. Reframe with context:
- High ACOS in a launch → "launch-phase investment" and pair with growth or impression numbers.
- Low absolute sales → lead with the growth multiple instead of the total.
- Negligible programs → drop the slide or card entirely.

---

## 8. Figure banding (High-Level report only)

The High-Level deck is shareable externally, so **private absolute $ figures are banded**:

| Real value | Band |
|---|---|
| $1,000–$3,999 | Low 4-Fig |
| $4,000–$6,999 | Mid 4-Fig |
| $7,000–$9,999 | High 4-Fig |
| $10K–$39K | Low 5-Fig |
| $40K–$69K | Mid 5-Fig |
| $70K–$99K | High 5-Fig |
| $100K–$399K / $400K–$699K / $700K–$999K | Low / Mid / High 6-Fig |
| $1M–$3.9M / $4M–$6.9M / $7M–$9.9M | Low / Mid / High 7-Fig |

Band: revenue, ordered sales, ad-attributed sales, ad spend, ASP, starting-point sales.
Keep real: percentages, multiples (5.2×), ROAS, impressions, units, ASIN/module counts.
If a context line leaks a $ figure, swap it for a ratio ("on $28.5K spend" → "1.3× blended ROAS").
In body copy write the band out in full: "a **Mid 5-Figure** first year".

The Detailed deck keeps exact figures (internal / QBR use).

---

## 9. Formatting conventions

- Currency: `$64.6K`, `$1.2M`, `$28.44` (one decimal for K/M, two for unit price).
- Multiples: `5.2×` (true multiplication sign ×, not x).
- Percent: `98.9%`, deltas with arrow: `▲ +62%` / `▼ −8%`.
- Periods: topbar short form `Sep 25 – Aug 26`; captions long form `Sep 2025 – Aug 2026`.
- Separator dot: ` · ` between label fragments.
- Slide counter: `NN / total` (e.g. `05 / 05`, `08 / 14`).
- Em dash `—` with spaces for asides; en dash `–` for ranges.
