## Vision

### 10x Check
Align the entire app architecture to the company org chart. Each department (SEO, Design, PPC, Catalog, Finances) becomes a first-class workspace with its own landing page, scoped activity feed, and hierarchical permissions. Cross-department connections are visible, not hidden. The app IS the company — navigating 1o8Hub feels like walking through the office.

## Two App Surfaces
- **1o8Hub** — internal, company members manage Amazon products
- **1o8Portal** — client-facing, isolated from Hub

## Permission Model (Hierarchical)

```
CURRENT:  allowedSections: ["seo", "aplus", "sales"]  (flat)

NEW:      allowedSections: ["seo", "design", "ppc"]   (department-level)
          OR granular:     ["seo", "design.aplus"]     (subsection-level)

Resolution: canAccess("design.aplus") checks:
  1. "design" in allowedSections (department grant)  → YES
  2. "design.aplus" in allowedSections (subsection)  → YES
  3. isAdmin                                          → YES
```

Shared utility: `src/lib/permissions.ts` (used by both sidebar and API routes).

## Scope Decisions

| # | Proposal                        | Effort | Decision | Reasoning                                             |
|---|---------------------------------|--------|----------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | Section Landing Pages           | M      | ACCEPTED | Turns departments from nav groups into workspaces     |
| 2 | Hierarchical Permissions        | S      | ACCEPTED | Natural extension for department-based access control |
| 3 | Cross-Department Badges         | S      | ACCEPTED | Makes subsection connections visible in UI            |
| 4 | Finances Section Bootstrap      | M      | ACCEPTED | New department needs at least a landing page          |
| 5 | Department-Scoped Activity Feed | S      | ACCEPTED | Each team sees only their relevant changes            |

## Implementation Order

1. Extract `canAccess` to shared `src/lib/permissions.ts`
2. Reorganize sidebar sections to match department structure
3. Add hierarchical permission resolution (dot-notation)
4. Add department landing pages (one per department)
5. Add cross-department badges
6. Bootstrap Finances section with cross-client finance view
7. Add activity feed department filter

## Security Notes
- Finance reports gated behind `finances` department permission
- Hierarchical permissions backward-compatible (flat strings still work)
- Cross-department badges respect access control (tooltip if no access)

## NOT in scope
- Moving file/route directories to match departments (too disruptive)
- Department-level notification preferences (future)
- Department budgets/headcount tracking (different system)
