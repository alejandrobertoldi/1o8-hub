## 2. The 1o8Portal

The 1o8Portal is your centralized hub for managing every aspect of your Amazon presence. You will receive login access to the app, where you will find the following sections:

- **Timeline**: A visual project roadmap showing our 90-day strategy, daily tasks, and key milestones — so you always know what's happening and what's coming next.
- **Sales**:
  - *Overview*: A real-time dashboard showing all your key sales metrics (units, revenue, PPC spend, ACoS) across custom date ranges — daily, weekly, and monthly.
  - *Forecast*: Sales and PPC projections to help plan inventory and budget ahead of time.
  - *Inventory*: A detailed inventory health view including stock levels, inbound shipments, sell-through velocity, and restock recommendations to prevent stockouts.
- **Products**: The "source of truth" for all your Amazon catalog — active listings, discontinued items, and new product entries. Every attribute and valid value lives here, ensuring alignment between our teams.
- **SEO**: Track the progress of your listing optimizations through our multi-phase pipeline — from keyword research and competitor analysis to final content generation and implementation.
- **Design**: Review and approve all creative assets — A+ Content modules, Brand Story, Storefront designs, and product images — with inline commenting for fast feedback.
- **Reports**: Access monthly performance reports, export data, and review historical trends all in one place.
- **Finance**: View your Amazon financial statements, settlement breakdowns, and revenue attribution data.
