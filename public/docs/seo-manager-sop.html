1. Purpose & scope

This SOP describes how a listing goes from research to approved, pushed content in SEO Manager. It covers the nine phases (S0–S8), who does what, which rules the content must follow, and how review and client approval work.

S0 Setup→S1 Intake→S2 Keywords→S3 Reviews→S4 Alexa for Shopping→S5 COSMO→S6 SEO Content→S7 Review & Share→S8 Push
Golden rule: generating content is never the same as approving it. Every phase moves forward only when someone reads the output and approves it.

3. Before you start

  • SEO access in the Hub (ask an admin if the SEO section is missing).
  • The client's brand guidelines (voice, audience, words to avoid, supported claims).
  • Access to Helium 10 Cerebro to export keyword CSVs, and the review CSV export for the parent ASIN.
  • Access to Amazon's Alexa for Shopping chat for S4.
  • Product facts: sizes, counts, materials, usage — anything a claim will rely on.

4. How the project page works

  • Everything on one page. Phases are stacked cards. Closed cards are slim pills; open cards show the work. The left menu lists phases and sub-steps; click to jump.
  • Unlocking. A phase card is locked until the phase before it is approved. Approving a phase is one click: the approved card closes and the next one opens.
  • S6 sub-sections (S6.1–S6.8) carry a LOCKED or UNLOCKED status. Locked = finished and protected; unlocked = editable.
  • AI model. The AI · Opus 4.7 pill in the header shows which Claude model the AI steps use (hover for the model per step).
  • Saving. Work saves as you go. If something new isn't showing after an update, do a full page reload.

5. S0 — Project Setup

  1. New Project in the Content Control Center.
  2. Select Client.
  3. Target ASINs — choose marketplace and the ASINs in scope (single ASIN, variation family or group).
  4. Content Rules — for each section (Title, Bullet Points, Description, Backend) choose shared (same for all variations) or dimension-specific (e.g. different per Size).
  5. Name & Assignee, then Confirm & Create.

Done when: the project exists with the right client, ASINs, rules and assignee.

6. S1 — Intake

  1. Project Summary — check client, brand, marketplace, ASINs and variations. Fix scope problems now.
  2. Variety Pack Details (if shown) — confirm contents and quantities.
  3. Baseline Analysis — choose the baseline listing version per ASIN; correct missing or wrong facts.
  4. Competitors — add the competitors that matter.
  5. Brand Guidelines — upload or paste. The full text is given to every AI step, so put real rules in it.
  6. Confirm & Approve.

Done when: the facts and guidelines are right — every later step builds on them.

Guidelines cascade — which guideline wins

Guidelines can be set at four levels. When two levels say different things about the same field, the more specific one wins:

Listing>Group>Variation>Brand
  • Listing — rules for one specific listing (ASIN).
  • Group — rules for a variation group the ASIN belongs to.
  • Variation — defaults for the variation family.
  • Brand — the free-text brand guidelines from S1; they apply to every field the typed levels above don't cover.

S6 shows which levels are set for the current batch in its guidelines panel. The batch currently resolves the cascade from the first ASIN of the project, so put ASIN-specific rules at the listing level of that ASIN or in the brand text.

7. S2 — Keywords (Cerebro CSV)

The direct Helium 10 connection is switched off. Keywords come from a Helium 10 Cerebro CSV that you export and upload.
  1. Step 1 — Copy ASINs into Cerebro. Use Copy variation ASINs (child ASINs; the parent is left out), paste into Cerebro, run the research, export CSV.
  2. Step 2 — Upload the Cerebro CSV. Supported columns: Keyword Phrase, Search Volume, Cerebro IQ Score, Competing Products, Title Density, CPR, Position.
  3. Optional: upload competitor Cerebro CSVs.
  4. Parsed Data — filter out irrelevant phrases.
  5. Review Keywords — run tiering; check tiers (T1 = most important), intent and negatives.
  6. Approve S2.

Done when: the approved keyword list reflects the product and is tiered.

8. S3 — Reviews (review CSV)

  1. Upload the review CSV for the parent ASIN (one file covers all variations). Required columns: id, text, date, title, rating.
  2. Alternative: Use Hub reviews if the Hub already holds reviews for these ASINs.
  3. Check the parsed reviews, run the AI analysis, review themes, praise, complaints and objections.
  4. Approve S3. No reviews at all? Use Skip — No Reviews Available. Never invent review evidence.

9. S4 — Alexa for Shopping

  1. Phase 1 — copy the question block into Alexa for Shopping for the parent ASIN; paste all five answers back; confirm the mapping.
  2. Phase 2 — generate strategic questions (or use the defaults); collect and paste answers per batch; confirm.
  3. Review Q&A insights — check accuracy, unanswered questions and claims that need evidence.
  4. Approve S4.

Alexa answers are research to verify, not proof that a claim is true.

10. S5 — COSMO

  1. Check the prerequisite panel (S1–S4 complete).
  2. Generate COSMO Map.
  3. Remove relationships that are unsupported or irrelevant.
  4. Approve S5.

Working in the S6 tables

  • Edit — click a text cell to edit it in place.
  • Lock — the lock icon in a cell's left margin protects that cell from regeneration. Regenerating a batch overwrites only unlocked cells of that batch.
  • Regenerate with feedback — type what to change (e.g. "BP4: don't use 'medicine'") and regenerate.
  • Compliance panel — lists critical terms and warnings with where they occur. Fix the text or regenerate; bypass only when you are sure the word is allowed.
  • S6.8 layout — one row per ASIN: Product (thumbnail, ASIN, variation details), Title / Item Highlight, Bullet Points (BP1–BP5 stacked), Product Description, Backend Search Terms — each with its length.
After submitting: S6 stays locked across reloads. To change it, use Unlock for Editing, make changes, and submit again. Submitting text identical to the approved revision is refused ("No changes since rN").

12. Keyword tables (S6.2–S6.5)

Below each batch, a table lists the approved keywords and search queries (Helium 10 volume and, when available, Amazon Search Query Performance data).

  • Same columns everywhere, always in this order: Title · Item Highlight · Bullets · Description · Backend — minus the current step's own. A tick means the keyword already appears in that part of the listing.
  • Live — the ticks are read from the current text, so when you generate Bullets, the Titles table's Bullets column fills in by itself.
  • First checkbox = in this step's text. Green rows start ticked: untick to remove the keyword on the next Regenerate. Red rows start unticked: tick to add it. Pending changes show "will add" / "will remove".
  • Sorting — click any header, including the first column (used in this step).

13. S7 — Review & Share with Client

S7 is where the content written in S6 is checked by the team and, usually, the client before anything is pushed. Every time S6 is submitted, the Hub freezes a copy of the text called a revision (r1, r2, r3…). Reviewers always look at that frozen copy, so S6 can't change under them.

SEO Manager submits→Project Manager→Account Manager→Client→Approved
No automatic notifications. The Hub does not email or message reviewers when it's their turn. After each step, tell the next person (PM, AM or client) yourself. Reviewers can also see what's waiting for them in the Content Control Center.

13.2 Submitting (SEO Manager)

  1. Finish S6.2–S6.7 and review everything in S6.8 Final SEO Content.
  2. Click Save & Submit for review. This creates the revision, sets it to PM review, and locks S6 (it stays locked after reloads).
  3. Tell the Project Manager it's ready.
  • Submitting text identical to the approved revision is refused: "No changes since rN — nothing to review".
  • To change content later, use Unlock for Editing in S6. Unlocking does not change any revision already in review.

13.3 Reading the review tracker

13.4 Reviewing the content (PM and AM)

  1. Open S7. Use the ASIN buttons to switch between products. The preview looks like an Amazon product page: listing images (Hub images first, Amazon images as fallback), title, item highlight, bullets and description. Keywords are highlighted; backend search terms are shown below the preview (team only).
  2. Comment: hover any field and click it; the comment thread opens right under it. Comments are internal unless written for the client.
  3. Edit on the spot: in the same box, change the text and click Save edit. The edit updates the revision and S6, and is logged as an internal note showing what was removed and added. Edit notes resolve themselves and don't count as open comments.
  4. Decide by ticking your box in the tracker (see 13.5).
FieldEdit limit in review
Title75 characters
Item Highlight125 characters
Bullet points 1–5200 characters each — saving (and sending a client suggestion) is blocked above it
Product Description1,200 characters

On-the-spot editing is available while the revision is in PM or AM review, to team members with editor rights or above. Once it's with the client, the team changes text only by accepting the client's suggestions.

Send to client or Approve as final? Send to client when the client normally signs off on listing copy. Approve as final only when the client already agreed outside the Hub (say so in the reason, e.g. "client approved by email 14 Oct"), or for internal or urgent changes. Approvals cannot be undone — to change approved content, start a new revision.

13.6 The client's side (portal)

  1. The client opens the review from the SEO section of their portal (it must be enabled for that client).
  2. They see the same Amazon-style preview with listing images and keyword highlighting. Backend search terms are hidden from clients.
  3. They can comment, and suggest edits: type new wording on a field and send it. They never change the text directly.
  4. They approve or request changes.

13.7 Handling client suggestions (team)

  • Each suggestion appears on its field in S7 with a before/after and a status: Pending review, Accepted or Declined.
  • Accept puts the client's wording into the revision and S6. Decline closes the suggestion without changing anything.
  • Handle all suggestions before the client's final approval, and tell the client what you accepted or declined.

13.8 Overrides and self-approval

StepAssigned personWho can override
Project ManagerThe client's PMThe client's AM or ES · any Owner
Account ManagerThe client's AMThe client's ES · any Owner
ClientThe clientAn Owner, on the client's behalf, with a written reason (never the person who submitted)
  • An override is shown as (override) in the tracker and history, so it's always visible who stood in.
  • Self-approval — approving a revision you submitted — is allowed only for the client's Account Manager or Executive Strategist, and is meant for emergencies. From the Project Manager downwards it is never allowed.
  • If the same person submitted and approved every step, the content has had no second pair of eyes. Prefer sending it to the client in that case.

13.9 When changes are requested

  1. The revision closes as Changes requested, with the reviewer's note.
  2. The SEO Manager opens S6 (Unlock for Editing), works through the open comments shown there, and fixes the content.
  3. Submit again: a new revision (r2, r3…) starts at PM review. The previous approved revision, if any, stays current until the new one is approved.

13.10 Checklist before you approve

  • Facts match the product (sizes, counts, materials, claims).
  • No critical prohibited terms; no competitor names.
  • Bullets ≤ 200 characters; backend ≤ 249 bytes.
  • Variation-specific details are right for each ASIN.
  • All comments answered; all client suggestions accepted or declined.

14. S8 — Push Content to Products

  1. S8 unlocks when a revision is approved; it pushes that approved revision, not whatever is currently in S6.
  2. Preview the push: matched and unmatched ASINs and the fields that will change.
  3. Confirm only when the preview is correct. Fix unmatched catalog records first.
Pushing updates the Hub product records and queues them for BigQuery publishing. It does not publish to Amazon by itself — follow the separate publishing process.

15. Limits & content rules

FieldLimit
Title≤ 75 characters; no colons
Item Highlight≤ 125 characters
Bullet pointsOne set of 5 · 180–200 characters each, never over 200 · ALL CAPS header + colon + normal body
Product DescriptionCharacter count shown; no colons
Backend Search TermsCounted in bytes; lowercase, no punctuation, no brand names
A+ Q&A (Module 7)5 pairs per ASIN · Question 115–120 · Answer 245–250 characters
  • No emoji; no competitor names; no keyword stuffing; never copy existing content verbatim.
  • Concise, Content and Key Features bullet versions are retired — only one bullet set is written.

16. Prohibited terms

One list drives both sides: the AI is told to avoid these words, and the S6 compliance check flags them as critical. Matching is whole-word ("best" does not flag "bestseller").

Medical / disease claims

cure, treat, heal, prevent, diagnose, disease, disorder, symptom, medical, medicine, drug, pharmaceutical, prescription, therapeutic, cancer, diabetes, antibiotic, clinically proven/tested/studied, doctor recommended, FDA / FDA approved…

Guarantee & result claims

guarantee(d), 100% guaranteed, money back guarantee, 100% effective, miracle, instant results, risk-free

Superlatives & rankings

best, #1, number one, top rated, award winning

Promotional & transactional

free shipping, in stock, buy now, add to cart, sale, discount, % off, click here, visit our website

When a term is flagged: edit the cell (fastest), or regenerate with feedback. Bypass only when the word is genuinely allowed for this product — Amazon's rules apply regardless.

17. Tracking projects

  • SEO Dashboard (/seo) — Client Health shows active clients only, with phase completion across S1–S8; the Phase Funnel shows where projects are; stale projects are listed last.
  • Client page and Content Control Center — each project card shows its ASINs on the left and, on the right, every phase with who worked on it and when, plus the S7 review stages and who pushed.

18. What changed in v2.0 (October 2026)

  • One-page project with S7 Review & Share and S8 Push as their own phases.
  • Keywords (S2) and reviews (S3) back to CSV uploads; the direct Helium 10 connection is off.
  • One set of 5 bullets; Concise / Content / Key Features retired.
  • Standard keyword tables across S6.2–S6.5; first column shows usage in the current step.
  • One prohibited-terms list for the AI and the compliance check.
  • Team and client review with tracker, on-the-spot edits, client suggestions and overrides.
  • S6 stays locked after submission; duplicate submissions refused; push uses the approved revision.
  • S6.7 uses all bullets, the full description and the full brand guidelines.
